Homeowners Insurance Cover
Defined Events (What Is Covered)
  • Fire
  • Lightning
  • Explosion
  • Storm, wind, water, hail or snow (excluding gates, fences & retaining walls)
  • Bursting, leaking or overflowing of water or heating installations or pipes
  • Theft or attempted theft — forcible & violent entry/exit required if building is let, lent or shared
  • Impact, including removal of fallen trees or branches following storm, wind, water or floods
Automatic Extensions — Included at No Extra Charge
  • Bereavement expenses — BWP 5,000 per insurance period following an insured event
  • House contents — BWP 5,000 per incident, up to BWP 10,000 per insurance period, following an insured event at the insured premises
  • Fire-fighting charges — up to BWP 40,000 per claim (10% excess, min BWP 2,000 applies)
  • Debris removal
  • Emergency accommodation while property is uninhabitable
  • Professional & rebuilding costs — demolition, debris removal, architects' and engineers' fees, local authority scrutiny fees
  • Glass & sanitary ware — accidental breakage of fixed glass (including mirrors) and fixed sanitaryware
  • Inflationary increase — sum insured automatically adjusted at renewal
  • Mortgagee clause — interest of any mortgagee protected
First Amounts Payable (Excess)
  • Basic excess: BWP 2,000 per claim
  • Subsidence & Landslip: 1% of sum insured, minimum BWP 5,000
Not Covered
  • Thatch roofs, non-standard buildings
  • Dwellings used for commercial purposes
  • Theft after building unoccupied 60+ consecutive days
  • Pre-existing damage
  • Malicious damage (deliberate/wilful acts)
  • Deliberate power cuts or load shedding
  • Loss caused deliberately by the insured
  • Faulty design or workmanship
  • Failure to maintain the property
  • Demolition, renovation or construction activities
  • Subsidence or landslip (separate excess applies)
  • TV aerials, masts or satellite dishes
  • Third-party liabilities
  • Property that remains physically undamaged
  • War, terrorism, civil commotion
  • Nuclear events
  • Communicable diseases (e.g. pandemics)
  • Cyber acts or cyber incidents
  • Failure of electricity grid / national power supply
  • Illegal occupation of the building
Average / Under-insurance: If the property is insured for less than its full replacement value, the insured bears a proportionate share of any loss. This condition applies to all property valued at BWP 500,000 and above.
