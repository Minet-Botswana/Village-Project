Terms and Conditions

By creating an account on the Kogae Village Homeowners Insurance portal, you agree that the information you submit is true, complete, and accurate. Providing false or misleading information may result in cancellation of your policy and forfeiture of all claims.

1. Misrepresentation & Non-Disclosure

Misrepresentation, misdescription or non-disclosure in any material particular shall render the affected section of your policy voidable. You are required to disclose all facts that are material to the insurance risk at the time of application and at renewal.

2. Use of Personal Information

Your personal data is collected and processed in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2024 (Botswana). It is used solely to process policy applications, verify identity, manage claims, and provide customer support. Your information will only be shared with legitimate and authorised parties required to administer your insurance contract, including the insurer B.I.C.B. Limited trading as Bryte Risk Services Botswana.

A full privacy statement is available at bryte.co.bw/legal/privacy-statement. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting Minet Botswana or your broker.

3. Communication

You consent to receive service-related communications by email, phone, or SMS regarding your policy, account activity, and claims. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

4. Premium Payment

Premium is due on or before the inception date or renewal date. Failure to pay by the due date may result in cancellation of cover at midnight on the last day of the preceding period of insurance, unless the failure was an error by your bank or paying agent.

5. Claims Obligations

In the event of a loss, you must notify us within 30 days and submit full written details of your claim within 30 days of the event. For theft or loss of property, you must inform the police as soon as practicable. Claims not pursued within 24 months of the event will lapse unless subject to pending legal action. If your claim is rejected in writing, you must serve legal process within 6 months of that rejection.

6. Fraud

Any fraudulent claim or use of fraudulent means to obtain a benefit under this policy will result in forfeiture of all benefits and cancellation of the policy.

7. Prevention of Loss

You are required to take all reasonable steps to prevent accidents or losses, including maintaining your property in good condition and complying with all applicable laws and regulations.

8. Sanctions

No cover, payment or benefit will be provided where doing so would violate any applicable trade or economic sanctions law or regulation.

9. Privacy & Account Security

Minet applies appropriate security measures to protect your information. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential. Notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

10. Policy Amendments

The insurer may amend the terms and conditions of your policy by providing 30 days' written notice to your last known address. Continued use of the portal constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

11. Governing Law

This agreement and all insurance contracts arranged through this portal are governed by the laws of the Republic of Botswana. The insurer is B.I.C.B. Limited trading as Bryte Risk Services Botswana, a registered insurance company (Registration No. BN2017/6844), Fairscape Tower, Gaborone.
