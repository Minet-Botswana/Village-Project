Motor Insurance Requires Homeowners Coverage

Motor insurance is available exclusively as an add-on to our homeowners insurance policies. You'll need to have an approved homeowners policy before you can apply for motor insurance.

How to Get Motor Insurance

  1. Apply for a homeowners insurance policy
  2. Complete the required documentation (KYC forms)
  3. Wait for homeowners policy approval from Minet
  4. Once approved, you can apply for the motor insurance add-on
