Motor Insurance Add-on Requires Homeowners Coverage

Motor insurance is available exclusively as an add-on to our homeowners insurance policies. You'll need to have approved homeowners coverage before you can apply for motor insurance protection.

How to Get Motor Insurance Coverage:

  1. Apply for homeowners insurance policy
  2. Complete the required documentation (KYC forms)
  3. Wait for homeowners policy approval
  4. Once approved, you can then apply for motor insurance add-on
