Claims Guidelines
How to submit and process your insurance claims
Claims Overview

At Minet Insurance, we understand that making a claim can be a stressful experience. Our claims guidelines are designed to help you through the process as smoothly as possible. We are committed to providing fair, fast, and transparent claims handling for all our policyholders.

Important Notice
All claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident. Delayed reporting may affect your claim processing.
Required Documents

To process your claim efficiently, please ensure you have the following documents ready:

  • 1
    Completed Claim Form
    Download and fill out the appropriate claim form completely and accurately.
  • 2
    Copy of Policy Document
    Provide a copy of your current insurance policy or policy number.
  • 3
    Proof of Loss/Damage
    Photos, videos, or other evidence of the incident or damage.
  • 4
    Police Report (if applicable)
    Required for theft, accidents, or criminal damage incidents.
  • 5
    Repair Estimates/Invoices
    Professional estimates for repairs or replacement costs.
  • 6
    Medical Reports (for personal injury)
    Medical certificates, hospital reports, and treatment records.
Claims Process Timeline
1. Report Your Claim
Contact us immediately to report your claim. Provide initial details about the incident.
2. Submit Documentation
Submit all required documents within 7 days of reporting your claim.
3. Claim Assessment
Our assessor will review your claim and may arrange for inspection if necessary (5-10 business days).
4. Claim Decision
We will notify you of our decision and settlement amount if approved (10-15 business days).
5. Payment Processing
If approved, payment will be processed within 5-7 business days of acceptance.
